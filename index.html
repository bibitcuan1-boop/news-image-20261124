Gaza · Lead Story

Israeli strike kills Hamas security chief in Gaza City

Naeem Abu Nahel, director of Hamas’s internal security apparatus, was killed in an Israeli airstrike in western Gaza City on Tuesday. Medics and police said at least six other people were killed in strikes across the territory the same day, including two police officers. Israel’s military said it had struck a senior Hamas militant.

Korean Peninsula

Russian jets near South Korean island trigger shelter alert

Several Russian military aircraft entered South Korea’s air defence identification zone on Tuesday morning and approached Ulleung Island. Seoul scrambled fighter jets and briefly told island residents to take shelter. The alert was lifted within minutes; the military said its airspace was not violated.

Ukraine

Overnight strike on Zaporizhzhia kills two, wounds 40

A Russian attack overnight hit residential areas of Zaporizhzhia, killing two people and wounding 40, including four children, the regional governor said on Tuesday. A five-storey apartment building was partly destroyed and a house levelled.

Iraq

Baghdad orders takeover of Kurdistan crossings and oilfields

Prime Minister Ali al-Zaidi ordered federal authorities to take over border crossings in the Kurdistan region and the oil ministry to manage oilfields under Kurdish security control in four provinces, after an oil-smuggling investigation at the North Oil Company.

Business

Sanofi agrees $41bn deal to buy US drugmaker Insmed

Sanofi said on Tuesday it had agreed to acquire Insmed for $175 a share in cash, a total of about $41 billion — the year’s biggest biopharma deal. The French drugmaker gains two marketed respiratory medicines and a late-stage pipeline.
